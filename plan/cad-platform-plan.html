PRD · Draft for product decisions

From CAD file to useful dimensions.
From dimensions to paid engineering work.

25 September 2026 · Working product name: CAD utility platform · “Vibfixture” retained as the user's term; branding unresolved.

1. Summary

A free, approachable CAD viewer brings users in through a practical job: drop a part, extract the dimensions that matter, and download an annotated image or PDF. Paid fixture design, drawing generation, and costing continue from that same part and its selected features. The initial product must earn trust through reliable measurements before expanding into engineering automation.

2. Product journey

Entry points: file-drop homepage and format-specific landing pages. Paid actions appear alongside the loaded part and after export; never interrupt the free export.

8. Production release gates

GateAcceptance evidence
Geometry accuracyCompare every supported measurement type against reference CAD values across a fixed corpus including units, transforms, small/large parts and curved features. Agree the numeric tolerance before implementation; all supported cases must pass.
Geometry authorityRendering tessellation and camera changes do not alter CAD-derived values. Mesh-derived values remain labeled in the viewer and every export. No silent fallback from CAD to mesh measurement.
Unit correctnessmm/in fixtures produce equivalent physical values; STL measurement cannot begin without confirmed units; decimal rounding is consistent in viewer and export.
Import robustnessSupported valid files load; malformed, unsupported, oversized, and multi-part inputs end in recoverable states. Partial imports cannot masquerade as complete models.
UsabilityProposed pilot target: at least 8 of 10 representative users independently import, obtain a specified dimension, and export within two minutes on an agreed sample part.
Export integrityPNG and PDF reproduce selected dimensions, units and orientation; labels are readable without clipping; exported values match the viewer.
PerformanceSet file/geometry limits and benchmark device/browser matrix during feasibility. Measure first usable view and responsiveness; publish limits and enforce cancellation. Numeric targets remain open until measured.
PrivacyNetwork inspection confirms free model data stays on-device if promised. Paid upload occurs only after consent; deletion and retention behavior match stated policy.
Paid fixture qualityExisting datum, concept and finalization gates remain intact. Separate CAD verification, fabrication readiness, calibration and inspection validation; package generation alone is not production approval.
Feasibility blocker to resolve first: Online3DViewer provides a viewer foundation, but the documented STEP importer exposes triangulated output. Exact CAD feature measurements require additional geometry access. Prove that access and acceptable local performance before committing to both “accurate production measurement” and “entirely local.”

10. Measures of success

MetricDefinition
Free activationSessions completing at least one measurement and export ÷ sessions with a successful supported import.
Time to valueMedian and 95th percentile time from file selection to first completed measurement, segmented by supported workload.
ReliabilityImport failures, measurement failures and export failures, each reported against its attempts.
Paid intentActivated sessions opening a paid service brief ÷ activated sessions; distinguish interest in unavailable services.
Paid conversion / economicsPurchased jobs ÷ qualified service briefs; track compute, engineering review time, revisions, refunds and margin per delivered job.
Paid outcomeJobs accepted by customer, revision count, delivery time and failure rate. Customer acceptance does not replace engineering verification.

Commercial targets and analytics consent approach remain open. Collect event categories and timings, not customer geometry or identifying file metadata.

11. Non-goals for the first release

  • Native SolidWorks/Inventor import, large assembly workflows, geometry editing, or a CAD replacement.
  • Automatic discovery of all critical dimensions, inferred tolerances, GD&T, or manufacturing certification.
  • Full engineering drawings, fixture generation or costing inside the free viewer.
  • Accounts, cloud libraries, collaboration, saved editable projects, and shared model links for free users.
  • Unbounded file support, full mobile editing, or all paid modules launching together.

12. Decisions still needed

  1. First paying customer and part family: welding workshops, checking/inspection users, or another segment? Supply representative, shareable parts for validation.
  2. Paid priority: fixture is the proposal; confirm whether drawing or costing should come first.
  3. Existing assets: identify the drawing and costing skills; confirm whether “Vibfixture” is the fixture module or umbrella brand.
  4. Precision and workload: agreed measurement tolerance, typical/max file complexity, supported browsers and benchmark hardware.
  5. Paid commercial model: per job, credits, subscription, or reviewed service; pricing, revisions, failed-job handling and turnaround.
  6. Engineering responsibility: who reviews paid outputs, which checks are automatic, and who authorizes fabrication or inspection use?
  7. Data policy: paid upload hosting, retention, deletion and any organizational restrictions.

13. Basis and references

  • User decisions in this discussion define the free acquisition tool and paid engineering services.
  • Online3DViewer repository: viewer/library foundation, supported formats and dependencies. Reuse is a proposal, not an audited production-readiness claim.

  • occt-import-js documentation: browser STEP import, unit options, triangulated output and face-to-triangle mapping.
  • Existing local design-fixture skill: weld/checking workflows, datum review, concept and finalization gates, exact CAD authority, and distinct verification statuses. Reviewed for product scope; no fixture job executed.
